Yong Hye-in, the nominee for Minister of Gender Equality and Family, resigned on the 13th. The Democratic Party of Korea then launched a full-scale defense of Kim Seung-won, the nominee for Minister of Justice. Recent polls showed significant public opinion deeming both nominees unfit, but criticism has been particularly intense toward Kim, as specific allegations of lobbying for the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety have emerged alongside controversies over small shareholder damages. Questions arise about whether these issues disqualify him from leading a ministry responsible for justice, fairness, and human rights.
